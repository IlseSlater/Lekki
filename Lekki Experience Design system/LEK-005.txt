EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==============================================================================

Every successful software platform develops its own language.

That language becomes the foundation upon which documentation,
architecture, software, support, onboarding and training are built.

Without a common language, different teams begin using different words for
the same concept.

This creates misunderstandings.

Duplicate functionality.

Inconsistent APIs.

Confusing documentation.

Poor user experiences.

The purpose of this document is to establish the official language of Lekki.

Every future discussion should use these terms consistently.

==============================================================================
WHY THIS MATTERS
==============================================================================

Imagine three conversations.

A developer says

"The table has moved."

The product owner says

"The session moved."

The customer says

"The guests changed tables."

The database says

"Location updated."

All four people may be describing exactly the same event.

The language removes ambiguity.

For Lekki every concept has one preferred name.

==============================================================================
CORE DOMAIN
==============================================================================

The highest-level business concepts are called Core Domain Objects.

These objects should remain stable over many years.

Organisation

Venue

Experience Profile

Guest

Staff Member

Session

Participant

Location

Order

Bill

Payment

QR Context

Service

Notification

These names should not change.

==============================================================================
ORGANISATION
==============================================================================

Definition

A legal business entity that owns one or more venues.

Examples

Ocean Hotels

ABC Hospitality

Sunset Festivals

Blue Mountain Wines

Responsibilities

Owns business data.

Owns staff.

Owns venues.

Owns reporting.

Owns billing.

Owns payment configuration.

Important Rule

Guests never belong to an organisation.

Guests visit organisations.

==============================================================================
VENUE
==============================================================================

Definition

A physical location operated by an organisation.

Examples

Restaurant

Hotel

Festival

Golf Estate

Casino

Airport Lounge

Conference Centre

Rules

A venue belongs to exactly one organisation.

A venue has one Experience Profile.

A venue contains Locations.

A venue exposes Services.

==============================================================================
EXPERIENCE PROFILE
==============================================================================

Definition

A reusable configuration that changes platform behaviour without changing
platform architecture.

Examples

Restaurant

Hotel

Festival

Conference

Future profiles may be added without modifying the platform.

Responsibilities

Terminology

Modules

Capabilities

Guest Journey

Business Rules

Visual Branding

Permissions

Experience Profiles are configuration.

Never software forks.

==============================================================================
LOCATION
==============================================================================

Definition

Any identifiable place inside a venue where interactions occur.

Examples

Restaurant Table

Hotel Room

Festival Zone

Golf Halfway House

Casino Table

VIP Lounge

Airport Gate

The platform should never assume a Location is a table.

Location is the generic concept.

==============================================================================
SERVICE
==============================================================================

Definition

Anything a venue offers to a guest.

Examples

Food

Drinks

Accommodation

Parking

Ticket Upgrade

Spa

Golf Booking

Wine Tasting

Room Service

Merchandise

Future services should require no architectural changes.

==============================================================================
GUEST
==============================================================================

Definition

A person interacting with a venue.

Important Characteristics

Global identity.

Platform account.

Cross-organisation history.

Personal preferences.

Payment methods.

Guests belong to the platform.

Not the venue.

==============================================================================
STAFF MEMBER
==============================================================================

Definition

A person performing work for an organisation.

Examples

Waiter

Manager

Receptionist

Cashier

Security

Bartender

Staff identities belong to organisations.

Not the platform.

==============================================================================
SESSION
==============================================================================

Definition

A temporary interaction between one or more guests and a venue.

Examples

Restaurant visit.

Hotel stay.

Festival attendance.

Wine tasting.

Conference registration.

The Session becomes the primary object around which interaction occurs.

Everything else relates to the Session.

==============================================================================
PARTICIPANT
==============================================================================

Definition

A Guest participating in a Session.

A Session contains one or more Participants.

Examples

Restaurant

Four guests sharing dinner.

Hotel

Two guests sharing one room.

Festival

Friends attending together.

Participant replaces customer-at-table terminology.

==============================================================================
ORDER
==============================================================================

Definition

A request made by one or more Participants for one or more Services.

Restaurant

Food order.

Festival

Drink purchase.

Hotel

Room service request.

Golf Club

Food at halfway house.

Order is venue agnostic.

==============================================================================
BILL
==============================================================================

Definition

The financial responsibility generated by one or more Orders.

Bills may be

Individual.

Shared.

Sponsored.

Transferred.

Merged.

Split.

The billing model should remain independent of venue type.

==============================================================================
PAYMENT
==============================================================================

Definition

A financial transaction settling all or part of a Bill.

Payments support

Card.

Tap-to-Pay.

Digital Wallets.

Cash.

Gift Cards.

Future payment methods.

Payment Providers are interchangeable.

Payments are platform concepts.

Providers are integrations.

==============================================================================
QR CONTEXT
==============================================================================

Definition

The information encoded or resolved from a QR code.

Context may include

Organisation.

Venue.

Location.

Language.

Experience Profile.

Campaign.

Permissions.

Capabilities.

A QR Code does not merely identify a web page.

It establishes context.

==============================================================================
CAPABILITY
==============================================================================

Definition

A reusable platform feature.

Examples

Ordering.

Payments.

Messaging.

Notifications.

Bookings.

Analytics.

Neo.

Capabilities may be enabled or disabled through configuration.

==============================================================================
MODULE
==============================================================================

Definition

A deployable unit of platform functionality.

Modules implement Capabilities.

Examples

Payments Module.

Ordering Module.

Analytics Module.

Neo Module.

==============================================================================
TENANT
==============================================================================

Definition

A technical implementation of an Organisation within the SaaS platform.

Business users should see Organisations.

Developers may refer to Tenants.

Documentation should prefer Organisation.

==============================================================================
EVENT
==============================================================================

Definition

Something meaningful that has already happened.

Examples

Session Started.

Participant Joined.

Order Submitted.

Bill Split.

Payment Completed.

Venue Closed.

Neo Recommendation Accepted.

Events become the foundation of future event-driven architecture.

==============================================================================
UBIQUITOUS LANGUAGE RULES
==============================================================================

Every concept has one preferred name.

Never invent synonyms.

Never create competing terminology.

Documentation.

Database.

API.

User Interface.

Support.

Analytics.

Neo.

All should use the same vocabulary.

Consistency reduces cognitive load.

==============================================================================
THE IMPORTANCE OF LANGUAGE
==============================================================================

The platform will continue evolving for many years.

New developers will join.

New industries will be supported.

New integrations will be added.

The common language established today becomes one of Lekki's most valuable
architectural assets.

Protect it carefully.

Whenever new concepts are introduced they should first become part of the
Ubiquitous Language before becoming part of the software.
