==============================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==============================================================================

Lekki is a next-generation venue platform designed to transform how guests,
staff and venue operators interact during a shared physical experience.

Unlike traditional hospitality software that is built specifically for a
restaurant, hotel or event, Lekki has been designed from the beginning to be
Venue Agnostic.

A venue is simply a place where people interact.

That place may be

• Restaurant
• Coffee Shop
• Sports Bar
• Music Festival
• Conference
• Hotel
• Casino
• Winery
• Golf Estate
• Stadium
• Theme Park
• Airport Lounge

Lekki provides one platform capable of adapting itself to every venue through
configuration rather than custom software development.

The objective is not to build another POS system.

The objective is to build the operating system for physical guest experiences.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MISSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Remove friction from every guest interaction.

Guests should never need to wonder

    "What app do I need?"

    "Where do I pay?"

    "Who do I ask?"

    "Which menu do I use?"

The venue should identify itself immediately after scanning a QR code and the
entire experience should adapt automatically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One application.

Every venue.

Any experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BACKGROUND
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lekki evolved from an existing production restaurant platform.

During development it became clear that most of the business logic was not
actually restaurant specific.

Examples include

• Guest sessions
• Orders
• Payments
• Notifications
• QR Codes
• Live updates
• User identities
• Analytics
• Staff workflows

Only the terminology changed.

A restaurant has tables.

A hotel has rooms.

A festival has zones.

A stadium has sections.

The interaction pattern remains almost identical.

Instead of building individual products for every industry, Lekki abstracts
the common behaviour into a configurable platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE PROBLEM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Today's venue software suffers from several common problems.

Every industry purchases different software.

Guests install different applications.

Payment experiences differ.

Staff require extensive training.

Integrations are duplicated.

Customer identities are fragmented.

Venue operators become locked into one vendor.

Lekki aims to solve these problems through a unified architecture.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE LEKKI APPROACH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lekki is based on six core ideas.

1.
Identity should belong to the guest.

Not the venue.

A guest should use one account across every venue.

2.
Experiences should be configured.

Not developed.

3.
The QR code provides context.

The application changes automatically.

4.
Offline capability is mandatory.

Not optional.

5.
Artificial Intelligence should assist people.

Not replace them.

6.
Existing business systems should integrate.

Not be replaced immediately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lekki believes software should disappear.

The guest should think about dinner.

Not technology.

The waiter should think about service.

Not software.

Managers should think about running their business.

Not configuring systems.

Technology exists to reduce cognitive load.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every architectural decision should satisfy at least one of these objectives.

Reduce friction.

Increase automation.

Improve guest experience.

Reduce staff training.

Reduce implementation effort.

Improve scalability.

Increase platform flexibility.

Preserve business knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOCUMENTATION STRATEGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Documentation is considered part of the product.

Every architectural decision must be documented.

Every business rule must be documented.

Every major design decision requires justification.

Every feature should explain

WHY it exists

WHAT problem it solves

HOW it works

HOW it evolves

This documentation should allow a new engineering team to understand the
entire platform without requiring tribal knowledge.

A successful implementation of Lekki will allow

Visit any participating venue.
