==============================================================================
PURPOSE
==============================================================================

This document defines every major business capability provided by the Lekki
platform.

A Capability is something the platform knows how to do.

Capabilities are independent of

Programming language

Microservices

Databases

User Interfaces

Technology

Capabilities describe business value.

Software simply implements them.

This distinction allows the platform to evolve without changing its business
architecture.

==============================================================================
WHAT IS A CAPABILITY?
==============================================================================

A Capability is a reusable business function.

Examples

Authenticate a Guest

Create a Session

Manage Billing

Process Payments

Generate Analytics

Configure a Venue

Capabilities should be reusable across every venue type.

Restaurant

Hotel

Festival

Casino

Conference

Airport Lounge

Golf Estate

Capabilities should not contain industry assumptions.

==============================================================================
CAPABILITY CHARACTERISTICS
==============================================================================

Every capability should satisfy the following principles.

Own one responsibility.

Publish events.

Subscribe to relevant events.

Remain independently deployable.

Avoid direct database access to other capabilities.

Expose well-defined contracts.

Support observability.

Be configurable.

Support Neo.

CAPABILITY DISCOVERY
==============================================================================

Every capability should answer

What problem do I solve?

Who owns me?

Which events do I publish?

Which events do I consume?

Which APIs do I expose?

Which data do I own?

Can Neo observe me?

Can I operate offline?

How am I configured?

==============================================================================
ARCHITECT'S NOTES
==============================================================================

Capabilities are the true building blocks of Lekki.

Microservices may change.

Frameworks may change.

Cloud providers may change.

Capabilities should remain stable.

When planning any new feature, the first question should never be:

"What service should we build?"

Instead ask:

"Which capability does this belong to?"

If the answer is unclear, the design is probably unclear.

A well-defined capability model ensures that Lekki continues to grow without
becoming a collection of disconnected features.
