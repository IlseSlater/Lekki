==============================================================================
PURPOSE
==============================================================================

Identity is one of the most valuable assets within the Lekki platform.

Rather than each organisation maintaining separate customer accounts,
Lekki provides a unified identity platform.

Guests authenticate once.

Their identity follows them across every participating organisation.

Businesses continue owning their operational data.

Guests own their digital identity.

==============================================================================

CORE PRINCIPLES
==============================================================================

Identity belongs to the Guest.

Not the Venue.

Not the Organisation.

Not the Device.

Authentication is global.

Authorisation is contextual.

Guest history is portable.

Business data remains isolated.

==============================================================================

PASSKEY FIRST
==============================================================================

Passkeys should become the preferred authentication method.

Advantages

No passwords.

Phishing resistant.

Device secure.

Fast sign-in.

Cross-device support.

Native browser support.

Excellent PWA compatibility.

The platform should encourage Passkeys by default.

==============================================================================

ARCHITECT'S NOTES
==============================================================================

Identity is far more than authentication.

Identity is the relationship between a Guest and the Lekki platform.

By separating identity from Organisations, Lekki creates a persistent digital
companion that follows Guests throughout their journey, while respecting
business ownership boundaries and privacy requirements.

This approach enables frictionless onboarding, seamless payments,
personalised experiences and intelligent assistance from Neo, all without
locking Guests into a single business.

Identity is the foundation upon which trust is built.
