==============================================================================
PURPOSE
==============================================================================

Experience Profiles are the primary mechanism that makes Lekki venue
agnostic.

Rather than building separate software products for restaurants,
hotels, festivals or casinos, Lekki delivers one configurable platform.

Experience Profiles determine how the platform behaves.

The underlying architecture never changes.

Only the experience changes.

This principle allows Lekki to expand into entirely new industries without
creating new applications.

==============================================================================
THE EXPERIENCE ENGINE
==============================================================================

The Experience Engine is responsible for adapting platform behaviour to
match a particular business environment.

The engine receives context from

Organisation

Venue

Location

QR Context

Guest

Capabilities

Business Rules

Based on this information the engine determines how the platform behaves.
