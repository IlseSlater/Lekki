EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==============================================================================

Lekki is not a restaurant application.

Lekki is not a hotel application.

Lekki is not a festival application.

Lekki is a configurable Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform that enables
businesses operating physical venues to create intelligent guest experiences
without developing custom software.

The commercial strategy is intentionally broader than hospitality.

Restaurants represent the first implementation of the platform because they
provide an ideal environment to validate the architecture, operational model,
guest experience and commercial viability.

Every design decision must support future expansion into additional industries
without requiring a rewrite of the core platform.

==============================================================================
BUSINESS VISION
==============================================================================

Lekki exists to become the preferred operating platform for physical guest
experiences.

Wherever people interact with businesses in a physical environment,
Lekki should be capable of managing that interaction.

The long-term objective is to provide a common digital layer between guests
and venues regardless of industry.

This creates a network effect.

The more venues that adopt Lekki,
the more valuable the platform becomes to guests.

The more guests that adopt Lekki,
the more valuable the platform becomes to venue operators.

==============================================================================
THE PLATFORM ECONOMY
==============================================================================

Traditional hospitality software creates isolated customers.

Every restaurant installs its own system.

Every hotel purchases another system.

Every venue creates another customer account.

Every guest repeats onboarding.

Every payment experience differs.

Lekki changes this model.

Instead of isolated software installations,
every organisation joins the same platform.

Guests move freely between organisations.

Businesses benefit from shared innovation.

The platform grows stronger as adoption increases.

==============================================================================
WHO IS THE CUSTOMER?
==============================================================================

There are two different customers.

Commercial Customer

The business purchasing Lekki.

Examples

Restaurant Owner

Hotel Group

Festival Organiser

Wine Estate

Golf Club

Casino

Conference Centre

Airport Lounge

Guest Customer

The individual interacting with the venue.

The guest does not purchase Lekki.

The guest experiences Lekki.

Every decision must balance the needs of both customers.

==============================================================================
WHO OWNS THE RELATIONSHIP?
==============================================================================

Businesses own their customer relationships.

Lekki owns the platform.

Guest identity belongs to the guest.

Business data belongs to the organisation.

Transaction history belongs to the organisation.

Platform capabilities belong to Lekki.

This separation is critical.

Examples

Within an organisation multiple venues may exist.

Example

Ocean Hotels

Cape Town Hotel

Durban Hotel

Johannesburg Hotel

Each venue operates independently while sharing
organisation-wide policies.

==============================================================================

Restaurant

Festival

Conference Centre

Casino

SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE
==============================================================================

Lekki will be delivered as SaaS.

Customers subscribe to the platform.

Infrastructure is managed centrally.

Updates are delivered continuously.

Customers always run supported versions.

Benefits include

Lower onboarding effort

Rapid feature delivery

Improved security

Central analytics

Lower operational cost

==============================================================================
COMMERCIAL MODEL
==============================================================================

The preferred pricing strategy is value-based.

Pricing should avoid charging per staff member.

Seasonal businesses frequently increase staffing levels.

Charging per user discourages adoption.

Preferred pricing options

Per Organisation

Per Venue

Platform Tier

Premium AI Services

Enterprise Integrations

Transaction Services

Marketplace Services

Additional capabilities may become optional modules.

==============================================================================
BUSINESS MODEL SUMMARY
==============================================================================

Lekki is building a platform.

Not an application.

Applications solve today's problem.

Platforms solve tomorrow's problems.

Every architectural decision should increase
the long-term value of the platform.

If a decision only benefits one venue type,
it should be challenged.

If a decision benefits every future customer,
it strengthens the platform.

This distinction defines Lekki's competitive advantage.
