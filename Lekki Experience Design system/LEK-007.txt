==============================================================================
PURPOSE
==============================================================================

This document defines the Domain Driven Design strategy used throughout the
Lekki platform.

The objective is not to follow DDD academically.

The objective is to organise the software around the business instead of
organising the business around the software.

Every important business concept should have a natural representation in the
codebase.

When the business changes,
the software should evolve naturally.

==============================================================================
WHY DOMAIN DRIVEN DESIGN
==============================================================================

Most enterprise systems slowly become collections of APIs,
database tables,
controllers and services.

After several years nobody understands where the business logic actually lives.

Rules become duplicated.

Features become inconsistent.

Development slows.

DDD prevents this by making the business domain the centre of the system.

Everything else becomes supporting infrastructure.

Business survives.

Technology changes.

==============================================================================

==============================================================================

The platform is built around one simple idea.

People interact with Venues.

Everything else supports that interaction.

This interaction creates

Sessions

Orders

Bills

Payments

Notifications

Analytics

Everything begins with a Session.

==============================================================================
BOUNDED CONTEXTS
==============================================================================

Lekki is divided into multiple business domains.

Each domain owns its own language,
rules,
models,
events,
and persistence.

The bounded contexts are

Identity

Organisation

Venue

Experience

Guest Session

Ordering

Billing

Payments

Neo

Analytics

Notifications

Administration

Integrations

Reporting

Future contexts may be added.

Existing contexts should remain independent.

Responsibilities

Staff Accounts

Venues

Payments

Not WHAT.

==============================================================================
AGGREGATES
==============================================================================

Large business concepts become Aggregates.

Examples

Organisation

Venue

Guest Session

Bill

Order

Payment

Each Aggregate protects its own consistency.

Other contexts communicate through APIs or events.

Never direct database access.

==============================================================================
ENTITIES
==============================================================================

Entities possess identity.

Examples

Guest

Venue

Session

Participant

Order

Bill

Payment

Identity remains constant.

Attributes may change.

==============================================================================
VALUE OBJECTS
==============================================================================

Value Objects possess meaning but no identity.

Examples

Money

Address

Coordinates

Business Hours

Language

Tax Rate

Currency

QR Context

Two equal Value Objects are interchangeable.

==============================================================================
DOMAIN SERVICES
==============================================================================

Some business logic belongs to no single Entity.

Examples

Bill Splitting

Venue Discovery

Pricing Rules

Recommendation Engine

Availability Calculation

These become Domain Services.

==============================================================================
APPLICATION SERVICES
==============================================================================

Application Services coordinate work.

Example

Guest scans QR

↓

Find Venue

↓

Load Experience

↓

Create Session

↓

Return Guest Context

Application Services orchestrate.

They do not own business rules.

==============================================================================
DOMAIN EVENTS
==============================================================================

Every meaningful business action becomes an Event.

Examples

VenueCreated

SessionStarted

ParticipantJoined

OrderSubmitted

OrderPrepared

BillCreated

BillSplit

PaymentCompleted

VenueClosed

NeoRecommendationAccepted

Events become the language between contexts.

==============================================================================
REPOSITORIES
==============================================================================

Repositories retrieve Aggregates.

Examples

VenueRepository

GuestRepository

SessionRepository

BillRepository

Repositories hide persistence.

Business logic never depends on SQL.

==============================================================================
FACTORIES
==============================================================================

Complex object creation belongs inside Factories.

Examples

Create Session

Create Venue

Create Experience

Create Bill

Factories guarantee valid initial state.

==============================================================================
SPECIFICATIONS
==============================================================================

Business rules become reusable Specifications.

Examples

CanJoinSession

CanCloseVenue

CanSplitBill

CanTransferParticipant

CanOrderAlcohol

CanCheckout

Specifications eliminate duplicated rules.

==============================================================================
ANTI-CORRUPTION LAYER
==============================================================================

External systems rarely speak Lekki's language.

POS

Accounting

Payments

ERP

Inventory

Each integration passes through an Anti-Corruption Layer.

Its responsibility is translation.

Never contamination.

Lekki should never inherit poor models from external systems.

==============================================================================
DEPENDENCY RULE
==============================================================================

Dependencies always point inward.

Infrastructure depends on Domain.

Never the opposite.

UI depends on Domain.

Database depends on Domain.

Neo depends on Domain.

Payments depend on Domain.

The Domain depends on nothing.

==============================================================================
THE GOLDEN RULE
==============================================================================

If the business disappeared,

the database schema would become meaningless.

If the database disappeared,

the business should still make sense.

Therefore...

Model the business.

Not the database.

==============================================================================
ARCHITECT'S NOTES
==============================================================================

A healthy domain model feels natural.

Business people recognise it.

Developers understand it.

Neo can reason about it.

Documentation reflects it.

The software becomes easier to evolve because the architecture mirrors reality
instead of implementation details.

Every future module added to Lekki should first ask:

"What business capability am I modelling?"

If that question cannot be answered clearly,
the feature probably does not belong in the domain.
