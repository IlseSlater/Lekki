==============================================================================
PURPOSE
==============================================================================

The Venue is one of the primary aggregates within the Lekki domain model.

A Venue represents a real-world place where guests interact with an
organisation.

Regardless of industry, every guest interaction occurs within a Venue.

Restaurants.

Hotels.

Festivals.

Golf Estates.

Casinos.

Conference Centres.

Airports.

Wine Farms.

Future industries.

The Venue provides a common operational model for all of them.

==============================================================================

THE ROLE OF A VENUE
==============================================================================

A Venue is responsible for

Hosting Guests

Hosting Sessions

Hosting Locations

Providing Services

Operating Staff

Executing Workflows

Collecting Payments

Generating Analytics

Producing Operational Events

The Venue does not own Guests.

The Venue temporarily serves Guests.

==============================================================================

VENUE STATES
==============================================================================

A Venue operates within one operational state.

Draft

Configuration is incomplete.

Ready

Configured but not accepting guests.

Open

Operational.

Busy

High operational load.

Offline

Operating locally.

Maintenance

Temporarily unavailable.

Closed

Operations finished.

Archived

No longer operational.

Neo continuously monitors venue state.

==============================================================================

ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS
==============================================================================

ADR-012-001

Venue is the operational boundary.

ADR-012-002

Guests visit Venues.

They do not belong to them.

ADR-012-003

Locations are generic.

Experience Profiles provide terminology.

ADR-012-004

Venue capabilities are configured.

Not developed separately.

ADR-012-005

Neo continuously observes every Venue.

==============================================================================

SUCCESS CRITERIA
==============================================================================

A newly created Venue should be operational within minutes.

The Venue should

Generate QR Codes

Configure Services

Accept Guests

Operate Offline

Collect Payments

Generate Reports

Receive Neo Recommendations

without software development.

==============================================================================

ARCHITECT'S NOTES
==============================================================================

The Venue is the heartbeat of Lekki.

Every guest interaction, operational process, payment, recommendation and
business insight ultimately flows through a Venue.

By designing the Venue as a configurable operational boundary rather than a
restaurant-specific concept, Lekki becomes capable of supporting virtually any
physical experience business.

This decision is foundational to the long-term vision of Lekki as a universal
Experience Platform.
