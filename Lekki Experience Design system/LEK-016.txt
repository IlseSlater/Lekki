==============================================================================
PURPOSE
==============================================================================

The Experience Runtime is responsible for executing the guest experience.

It receives context from the QR Context Engine, Identity Platform and Session
Manager before determining what the guest should see and what actions are
available.

Rather than embedding business logic inside user interfaces, Lekki centralises
runtime decision making inside the Experience Runtime.

This allows one platform to deliver unlimited venue experiences.

==============================================================================

VISION

==============================================================================

The Experience Runtime is the conductor of the platform.

It does not own Orders.

It does not own Payments.

It does not own Sessions.

It coordinates them.

Every guest interaction passes through the Runtime.

ARCHITECT'S NOTES

==============================================================================

The Experience Runtime is the execution engine of Lekki.

The QR Context Engine provides context.

The Identity Platform provides identity.

The Session Manager provides operational state.

The Experience Profile defines behaviour.

The Runtime brings all of these together into a single coherent guest
experience.

This architecture removes hardcoded user journeys and replaces them with
configuration, orchestration and context.

It is this layer that enables Lekki to remain venue agnostic while still
delivering highly tailored guest experiences across every industry.

Chief Architect's Recommendation

At this point, I think we've uncovered what I now see as the five architectural pillars of Lekki:

Experience Profiles – What kind of business is this?
QR Context Engine – Where am I?
Identity Platform – Who am I?
Guest Session – What am I currently doing?
Experience Runtime – What should happen next?

Together, these five components form the platform's "operating system" for guest experiences.
