==============================================================================
PURPOSE
==============================================================================

The Guest Session is the central business aggregate of the Lekki platform.

Every interaction between a guest and a venue occurs within a Session.

Rather than modelling restaurants around tables, hotels around rooms or
festivals around tickets, Lekki models every guest interaction as a Session.

This creates one consistent architecture across every industry.

Everything that happens during a visit belongs to a Session.

==============================================================================
THE CORE IDEA
==============================================================================

Guests do not interact directly with Orders.

Guests do not interact directly with Bills.

Guests do not interact directly with Payments.

Guests participate in Sessions.

Orders happen inside Sessions.

Bills belong to Sessions.

Payments settle Sessions.

Neo observes Sessions.

Analytics analyse Sessions.

The Session becomes the operational centre of the platform.

==============================================================================

A Session contains one or more Participants.

A Participant is a Guest participating in the Session.

Examples

Four friends dining together.

Family sharing a room.

Festival

==============================================================================

ARCHITECT'S NOTES
==============================================================================

The Guest Session is the beating heart of the Lekki platform.

Nearly every capability—ordering, billing, payments, notifications,
analytics, Neo, reporting and offline synchronisation—either contributes to
or derives value from a Session.

By making the Session the primary aggregate rather than industry-specific
concepts such as tables or rooms, Lekki achieves something very few platforms
have accomplished: one elegant operational model that spans every type of
physical guest experience.

This architectural decision dramatically simplifies future expansion while
providing a richer model for collaboration, intelligence and operational
optimisation.
