## Core Rules

- ALWAYS answer the three HCI questions on every guest view:
  1. Where am I?
  2. What can I do?
  3. What happens next?
- NEVER expose Studio, Platform, or Pack machinery to the guest.
- One question · one primary action · same Experience Shell for Live Experience.
