# Lekki voice

**Feel:** luxury hotel concierge disguised as software.  
**Pillars:** Confidence · Calm · Hospitality · Continuity.

## Register by surface

| Surface | Speak as | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Guest | A host who already knows why you are here | “You’re with us. Order when you’re ready.” |
| Studio | A calm installer. One question. | “Who is this experience for?” |
| Operate | A sous-chef under pressure. Short verbs. | “Serve next — Table 4” |
| Grow | A trusted manager at 7am. One number. | “Yesterday was quieter than usual. Two tables waited on mains.” |
| Marketing | Premium, specific, not startup-hype | “Go live in minutes. Guests already know what to do.” |

## Prefer / avoid

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|--------|
| Welcome, ready, with you, next, taken care of | Dashboard, manage, configure, submit, users |
| Table, pass, board, guests | Tenant, schema, runtime, pack, connector |
| What’s off? (86) | Inventory exception / OOS flag |
| Go live | Publish / deploy to production (in Studio) |
| Sign in | Authenticate |

## Gold

One primary action word. Verb + outcome: “Go live”, “Serve next”, “Pay the rest” — not “Submit”.
