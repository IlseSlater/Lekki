## Intent

Against most SaaS. Grow is almost invisible.

```text
Good evening.

You welcomed 42 guests today.

Tonight you took R12 400.

Most guests ordered the Burger.

Average wait
6 minutes

Guests were delighted.

──────────────

One suggestion

Open another station on Friday evenings.
```

That’s it.

---

## Principles

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Greeting · human sentences · few numbers | Revenue **grids** · visitor charts · dashboards |
| One trading figure in prose (calm breath) | Chart galleries · filter bars · export toolbars |
| One suggestion max | Insight feed · recommendation carousel |
| Feels like a trusted manager | Feels like BI / Excel |
| Link back to Operate when live | Trap the user in analytics |

---

## Studio vs Guest (vision)

Studio Grow hosts **owner confidence** (calm truth).  
Guest Experience hosts payment social (claim/split · loyalty) when Continuity craft is ready — not inside Grow.
