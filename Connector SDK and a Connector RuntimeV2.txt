What I Would Add (This Is the Part I'm Most Excited About)

I think connectors shouldn't just expose APIs.

They should expose business capabilities.

For example, the Pilot connector shouldn't say:

POST /payments

Instead it declares:

Capability

Create Payment

Capability

Refund

Capability

Settlement

Capability

Merchant Lookup

Then the Workflow Engine doesn't care whether it's Pilot, Stripe, Adyen, or NB.

It simply asks:

"I need someone who can Create Payment."

The Runtime selects the installed connector that provides that capability.

That means if a restaurant switches from Pilot to NB, their workflows don't change.

Only the connector changes.

I think that's a significant architectural improvement over most integration platforms. Instead of integrating with vendors, Lekki integrates with capabilities. Vendors implement those capabilities through connectors. That keeps the platform loosely coupled, makes workflows reusable, and makes it much easier for customers to change providers without redesigning their automations. I would absolutely make that one of the core design principles of the Integration Platform.

==============================================================================
PURPOSE
==============================================================================

This document defines the Integration Platform that enables Lekki to connect
to external systems without requiring custom software development.

Rather than building point-to-point integrations between systems, Lekki
provides a configurable Integration Engine built on top of the Event Bus.

The Integration Platform consists of three major components:

• Integration Engine
• Workflow Engine
• Connector Marketplace

Together these components allow customers to automate business processes,
connect third-party services and orchestrate operations from within Lekki.

This architecture is inspired by the strengths of products such as

• SnapScan
• Stripe Connect
• Zapier
• Make.com
• Shopify Apps
• Microsoft Power Automate

while remaining deeply integrated into Lekki's domain model.

LONG-TERM VISION
==============================================================================

The Integration Platform should eventually become a product in its own right.

Customers should never require external automation tools for normal business
operations.

The majority of hospitality workflows should be configurable directly inside
Lekki.

As the platform grows, partners should be able to publish connectors,
workflow templates and industry accelerators through the Marketplace,
creating an ecosystem around the Lekki Experience Platform.

==============================================================================
ARCHITECT'S NOTES
==============================================================================

The Integration Platform transforms Lekki from an application into an
orchestration platform.

Instead of simply processing transactions, Lekki becomes responsible for
coordinating people, systems, payments, documents and intelligence across the
entire business.

This architecture creates a significant competitive advantage because it
combines event-driven automation, hospitality-specific workflows and AI-driven
configuration into a single platform.

Over time, organisations will spend less time integrating software and more
time improving guest experiences.
